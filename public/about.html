About

A developer who ships.

I'm Arbin — a full-stack developer focused on web apps that solve real problems. I've shipped three live products and I'm still building: a media platform, a small focused web experience, and a project exploring how AI fails.

I work across the stack. On the frontend that means React, Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind — clean layouts, fast loads, and interaction that feels considered. On the backend: Node, Python, Firebase, Cloudflare Workers.

I care about the details people feel but don't see — spacing, motion, the weight of a font. Interfaces should disappear; the work should be what's left.
